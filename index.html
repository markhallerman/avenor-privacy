Dayvara

Your day, clearly arranged.

Dayvara is a calm, beautiful calendar for iPhone. It shows your Apple and Google calendars together, with fast day, week, month, and list views and a focused editor for creating and changing events.

Dayvara works entirely on your device. It has no servers of its own and never sends your calendar data to the developer. When you connect a Google account, Dayvara talks directly to Google on your behalf.
