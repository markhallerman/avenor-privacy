# Avenor Calendar Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Avenor Calendar is designed to respect your privacy.

## Data Collection

Avenor Calendar does not collect personal information, create user accounts, or track you across apps or websites.

Avenor Calendar does not maintain its own servers and does not send your calendar, event, or reminder data to the developer or to any third-party servers.

In Apple’s App Store privacy labels, Avenor Calendar is reported as “Data Not Collected.”

## Calendar Access

Avenor Calendar uses Apple EventKit to access the calendars and events you allow through iOS permissions.

Calendar access is used only to:

- show your calendar events in Avenor Calendar,
- create new events when you choose to add them,
- edit events when you choose to modify them, and
- delete events when you choose to remove them.

Your calendar data remains on your device and in the calendar accounts you already use, such as iCloud, Google Calendar, or other calendars configured in iOS.

The developer cannot access your calendar data.

## Reminders Access

Avenor Calendar can optionally show reminders alongside your calendar events.

Reminders access is used only when you choose to show reminders in Avenor Calendar. Turning off Show Reminders hides reminders in Avenor Calendar without changing your Reminders permission or modifying your reminder lists.

Your reminders remain on your device and in the reminder accounts you already use.

The developer cannot access your reminder data.

## Notifications and Alerts

Avenor Calendar does not use its own push notification service for event alerts.

When you create or edit event alerts, those alerts are saved as Apple EventKit alarms and are delivered by Apple Calendar and iOS.

## Analytics and Tracking

Avenor Calendar does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting services, advertising SDKs, or trackers.

Avenor Calendar does not track you across apps or websites.

## Network Access

Avenor Calendar does not send your calendar, event, or reminder data to the developer or to any third-party server.
